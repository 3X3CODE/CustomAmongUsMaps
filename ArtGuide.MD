# Among Us Art Style
- this is probably some secret info that most AU artists wont reveal.
* Use thick outlines.
* Make sure your objects have atleast a Main Color, a Shading Color (shadows) and an outline color. You can add additional colors like highlight in a suitable way.
* Make sure your colors match the purpose.
* When making darker colors, dont just lower the values on each RGB channel, you can shift them by a little to enhance the final outcome.
* This is based on preference, but my reccommendation is to not use a vector art program. You may instead use a pixel based one and use a larger canvas ( 3000px - 4000px, depends on room size ), draw one room at a time and put them together later.
# Map Background Characteristics
- To make sure your backgrounds and rooms look nice, ensure it has the floor and the wall.
- Make sure to have the horizontal wall height, horizontal door spacing, vertical door height and vertical door spacing the same throughout to you dont run into sizing issues when placing doors and other assets.
- What wall height to use?
* when choosing your wall height, the commonly used method is by placing the among us character knee height against the bottom outline and place the top outline where the top of the head sits.
- Floor designs? 
* when selecting a good design for your floor, the simplest ways are,
* Lines, Square tiles, Triangle patterns, Highlight and Shadow dots to indicate carpet.
* It is recommended to not use a too bright and saturated of a color.
* It is recommended to not have plain color floors.
* When drawing your floors, it may seem a bit too unnatural(colors, style, etc), in that case you can switch out the floor design and try out different stuff.
- Objects
* Objects follow the simple colors of a main color, outline color and shading color and optionally, a highlight color.
* the outlines of objects should be the thickness of your map outlines or larger.
* The color of you outline should look distinguishable from its background.
* These are some color patterns you can use, 
* low saturated blue/green/red - steel/metal
* The BIGGEST rule here when drawing objects in among us, is to NEVER make the objects too realistic. The more details you add to an object to make it similar to a real life thing, it looks unnatural to the among us artstyle and takes alot more for the user to process. Keep stuff simple!

# Export
* You assets should be seperated as static backgrounds with no interaction(blank areas for doors), animated objects in frames(should be excluded from the static background), interactable objects(consoles, cameras etc), doors(door open static image, door closed static image, door opening animation frames, door closing animation frames; should be repeated for both Horizontal and Vertical doors), and any other functioning object you'd like to implement in unity.
